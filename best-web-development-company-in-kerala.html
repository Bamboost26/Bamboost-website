Why Your Website Isn't Bringing in Enquiries

A website can look good and still lose customers. These are the problems we find most often when businesses come to us:

It loads too slowly.

Visitors leave before the page appears, especially on mobile data.

It's hard to use on a phone.

Small text, broken layouts and buttons that are hard to tap.

Google can't find it.

Built without SEO basics, so search engines struggle to read and rank it.

No clear next step.

No call, WhatsApp or enquiry button where visitors look for one.

It's hard to update.

Every small change needs a developer, so the site quickly goes out of date.
