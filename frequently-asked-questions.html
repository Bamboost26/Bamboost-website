Category 01

SEO & Search Engine Optimization

Questions about how Google Search works, what SEO involves, and what it can realistically deliver for your business.

SEO (Search Engine Optimization) builds organic rankings that generate traffic without paying per click - results compound over months and continue indefinitely. Google Ads places paid listings at the top of search results immediately but stops delivering traffic the moment your budget ends.

Most growing businesses benefit from both: Google Ads for immediate leads while SEO builds sustainable long-term visibility. Running both simultaneously provides complete search coverage and valuable data each channel can share with the other.

Google ranks websites based on hundreds of signals. The most significant are content relevance to the search query, the quality and quantity of backlinks pointing to the page, technical website performance (page speed, Core Web Vitals, mobile usability), and user engagement signals like click-through rate and time on page.

A structured SEO strategy works on all of these factors simultaneously - ensuring Google can find, crawl, understand, and trust your pages enough to rank them prominently for the searches that matter to your business.

Organic traffic is the visitors who find your website through unpaid Google Search results - not ads, not social media, not paid links. It matters because these visitors have active purchase intent, it costs nothing per click once rankings are achieved, and it compounds over time.

Businesses with strong organic visibility consistently generate the highest-quality leads at the lowest long-term cost per acquisition. SEO services that grow organic traffic are therefore among the highest-ROI digital marketing investments a business can make.

Keyword research identifies the exact search terms your target customers type into Google when looking for your product or service. It reveals search volume, competition level, and commercial intent - allowing us to prioritize the keywords most likely to drive qualified traffic and revenue.

Without proper keyword research, websites risk optimising for searches that don't convert, or targeting keywords too competitive to rank for at their current domain authority. Every Bamboost SEO campaign starts with a comprehensive keyword research and intent-mapping stage before any optimization work begins.

Yes - new websites can and do rank on Google, but they face a trust-building period. Most new sites begin ranking for lower-competition, long-tail keywords within 3 to 6 months of a structured SEO campaign. Competitive industry keywords typically take 6 to 12 months.

Starting with strong technical SEO, quality content targeting realistic keywords, and an early link-building strategy significantly accelerates the timeline. For faster initial visibility while organic authority builds, we often recommend running Google Ads in parallel.

A Google penalty is a manual or algorithmic action that reduces your website's rankings as a result of violating Google's webmaster guidelines. Common causes include spammy or purchased backlinks, keyword stuffing, duplicate content, cloaking, and hidden text. Manual penalties appear in Google Search Console; algorithmic penalties are harder to detect.

Both result in significant ranking drops requiring structured remediation. Bamboost uses exclusively white-hat SEO techniques to ensure every client's rankings are built on a penalty-free foundation. If your site has existing penalties, our technical audit will identify and remediate them.

Domain authority (DA) is a predictive score (1–100) that measures how likely a website is to rank in search results based on its backlink profile quality. Higher-authority domains rank more easily for competitive keywords and see better results from on-page optimization.

Domain authority is built by earning high-quality backlinks from authoritative, topically relevant websites over time through link building services, guest posting, digital PR, and brand mentions. It cannot be purchased quickly - it is the compounding result of consistent, quality-focused off-page SEO work.

E-E-A-T stands for Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, and Trustworthiness - Google's quality evaluation framework used by human quality raters. It assesses whether content is produced by someone with genuine experience and expertise, from authoritative sources, and whether the website is trustworthy.

Websites in healthcare, finance, legal, and other sensitive niches face the strictest E-E-A-T scrutiny. Building E-E-A-T signals requires author credibility indicators, accurate well-cited content, strong brand signals, and a technically trustworthy website. Bamboost builds E-E-A-T into every content and on-page SEO strategy.

Content marketing is the creation of valuable, informative content - blog posts, guides, comparisons, case studies - that attracts your target audience through Google Search. It supports SEO by targeting long-tail informational keywords, building the topical authority that helps all your pages rank, and earning natural backlinks from websites that reference your content.

Businesses with consistent content strategies compound their organic authority faster than those relying solely on service page optimization. Bamboost develops content strategies that align with keyword opportunity maps and business growth goals - not generic blog schedules disconnected from SEO impact.

GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) is the emerging practice of optimising your website and content to appear inside AI-generated responses from platforms like ChatGPT, Google Gemini, and Perplexity - rather than traditional Google blue-link results. Where SEO targets Google's ranking algorithm, GEO targets how AI language models retrieve and recommend information.

As AI-powered search continues to grow in usage, GEO is becoming an important component of complete digital visibility strategy. Bamboost integrates GEO alongside traditional SEO services to ensure clients maintain visibility across both traditional and AI-powered search channels.

Social media activity is not a direct Google ranking factor - social shares do not directly improve rankings. However, it indirectly supports SEO in important ways: it amplifies content to audiences who may link to it naturally, it drives referral traffic and increases online visibility for your brand, and a visible social presence contributes to the brand authority indicators Google increasingly incorporates into rankings.

The most efficient path to better rankings remains a combination of strong on-page SEO, quality link building, and technical performance - social media supports and amplifies these but does not replace them.

Effective SEO produces verifiable improvements in Google Search Console: keyword positions rising, impressions and organic clicks increasing over time. Your Google Analytics organic sessions should grow. Domain authority should trend upward. Healthy reporting shows specific keyword ranking data with Google's own tools - not vague activity summaries with no measurable outcomes.

If your agency cannot show measurable ranking improvements after 6 months using data from Google's tools, the campaign needs reassessment. Bamboost's performance-based model means our fee is directly tied to delivering measurable ranking improvements - contact us for a free audit of your current SEO situation.

Category 02

Web Development & Website Performance

Questions about building websites, choosing platforms, improving performance, and understanding what makes a website work for search and conversion.

A website redesign updates the visual design, layout, and user experience while keeping the existing content structure largely intact. A rebuild starts from scratch - new architecture, new codebase, often a new CMS platform. Rebuilds are appropriate when the existing site has fundamental technical problems that cannot be fixed within the current structure.

Both require careful SEO migration planning to preserve existing Google rankings. Bamboost manages website redesigns and rebuilds with a dedicated technical SEO audit before and after launch - ensuring no ranking equity is lost during the transition.

Page speed is a confirmed Google ranking factor. Slow websites rank lower and lose visitors before they engage - 53% of mobile users abandon pages taking more than 3 seconds to load. Every second of load time improvement measurably increases conversion rates. Google's Core Web Vitals are now part of the Page Experience ranking signal, making speed a double-priority for both ranking and revenue.

Our technical SEO services and web development both prioritise page speed optimisation at the code level - delivering verified improvements in Google PageSpeed Insights scores that benefit rankings and user experience simultaneously.

WordPress is an excellent choice for SEO when properly configured - it has strong plugin ecosystems (like Yoast and RankMath), clean URL structures, and flexible content management. However, the best CMS depends on your requirements: WordPress suits content-heavy sites, Shopify is purpose-built for ecommerce, Webflow suits design-forward businesses, and custom builds suit complex applications.

Bamboost recommends CMS platforms based on your specific goals, content workflow, expected growth, and technical requirements. We build on WordPress, Shopify, Webflow, and custom stacks - whichever best serves your long-term digital strategy. Learn about our web development services →

Mobile-first indexing means Google primarily uses the mobile version of your website to determine your rankings for all devices - desktop and mobile alike. If your mobile site is slow, has missing content, or renders poorly on small screens, your rankings for all searches are negatively affected, even for users searching on desktop computers.

This is why mobile performance is treated as a first-priority concern across both our technical SEO services and website development engagements. Every website we build or optimise is designed mobile-first, then scaled to desktop - exactly aligned with how Google indexes and evaluates sites.

Structured data is code added to your web pages that tells Google Search exactly what type of content each page contains - a product, a business, a FAQ, a review, an event. When Google understands your content precisely, it can display enhanced search results (rich results) including star ratings, FAQ dropdowns, price ranges, and product images directly in search results.

Rich results improve click-through rates and visual prominence significantly. Bamboost implements schema markup as part of both technical SEO and web development projects - ensuring your listings stand out in Google Search with rich result enhancements your competitors may not have.

A homepage introduces your entire business - who you are, what you offer, your credibility. A landing page is designed for a single conversion goal: capturing a lead for one specific service, converting visitors from one specific ad campaign, or ranking for one specific keyword cluster.

For Google Ads campaigns, dedicated conversion-optimised landing pages consistently outperform homepages because they match the visitor's specific search intent exactly. For SEO, location-specific and service-specific landing pages allow you to target precise keyword combinations that a single homepage cannot efficiently cover.

Category 03

Google Ads & Paid Search Advertising

Questions about how Google Ads works, campaign types, budgeting, and how paid search fits into a complete digital marketing strategy.

Google Ads targeting shows your ads based on the exact keywords people search (Search Ads), their demographic profile and interests (Display Ads), their past visits to your website (Remarketing), or machine-learning signals across multiple channels (Performance Max). You control geography, device, time of day, audience segments, and bidding strategies.

This precision ensures your budget reaches people with demonstrated intent. Our Google Ads management service builds targeting structures designed to minimise wasted spend while maximising the volume of qualified leads from your daily budget.

Quality Score is Google's 1–10 rating of your ad's relevance to the keyword it targets, the expected click-through rate, and the quality of your landing page experience. A higher Quality Score lowers your cost per click and improves your ad position - meaning better-quality ads pay less and rank higher than lower-quality competitors bidding the same amount.

Optimising Quality Score is one of the highest-impact ways to improve Google Ads ROI without increasing your budget. Bamboost focuses heavily on ad copy relevance, keyword match types, and landing page quality - three factors that directly raise Quality Scores and reduce your cost per lead. See our Google Ads services →

Remarketing shows ads specifically to people who have previously visited your website but didn't convert. These warm audiences have already demonstrated interest in your business - making them significantly more likely to complete a purchase or enquiry than cold audiences who have never encountered your brand.

Remarketing typically delivers higher conversion rates at lower cost-per-acquisition than standard campaigns. For most businesses, it is one of the most efficient uses of advertising budget. Our Google Ads management always includes remarketing setup and optimisation as a core campaign component alongside search campaigns.

Running SEO and Google Ads simultaneously is usually the most effective strategy - they operate on different timelines and serve complementary purposes. Ads deliver qualified traffic and leads immediately. SEO builds compounding organic authority that reduces long-term cost per acquisition. Ads data reveals which keywords convert best, directly informing SEO priorities.

Most businesses with limited budgets start with Google Ads for immediate traction, then layer in SEO to reduce ad dependency over time. Bamboost manages both services under one integrated strategy, ensuring each channel reinforces rather than duplicates the other.

Google Ads works best for businesses where customers actively search before purchasing. In Kerala, we have delivered strong results for real estate developers, educational institutions, healthcare providers, legal and professional services, ecommerce businesses, home renovation companies, and B2B service providers.

The key indicator is purchase intent - if customers in your market type specific service queries into Google when they need what you offer, Google Ads can place you at the top of those results immediately. Contact us to discuss whether Google Ads is the right channel for your specific business and market.

Questions about digital marketing strategy, how to choose an agency, what to expect working with Bamboost, and how we measure results.

Conversion rate optimisation (CRO) improves the percentage of website visitors who take a desired action - a phone call, an enquiry form submission, or a purchase. It involves analysing user behaviour, testing page layouts, improving calls-to-action, reducing friction in booking or checkout flows, and aligning page content with visitor intent.

Bamboost integrates CRO thinking into every web development and SEO engagement. There's no value in ranking well or running ads if the resulting website traffic doesn't convert into customers - so conversion improvement is built into every project, not treated as an optional add-on.

Before hiring an agency, verify they can show documented results from real clients using Google's own tools (Search Console, Analytics) - not proprietary metrics only they can interpret. Check that they communicate clearly without excessive jargon, that pricing is transparent without hidden retainer fees, and that they don't promise guaranteed #1 Google rankings, which no ethical agency can guarantee.

Most importantly, assess whether they understand your specific industry and local market. A generic template approach applied without industry knowledge produces mediocre results. Learn about Bamboost's approach and see our documented case studies before making your decision.

Reports include specific keyword position changes, organic traffic trends, domain authority progression, and activities completed each month.

Every metric in our reports is backed by data from Google's own tools - not proprietary scores only we can verify.
