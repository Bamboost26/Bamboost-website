# Bamboost Phase 1 — SEO & Website Redesign Strategy

## Objective
Redesign and optimize the existing Bamboost website for the initial launch.

- Optimize current pages only
- Remove duplicate/repetitive content
- Improve SEO structure, UX, and visual design
- Keep existing URLs
- Do not create new pages
- Do not perform a URL migration

## Service Positioning
Homepage:
- SEO = 50%
- Web Development = 50%

Dedicated service pages must remain strongly focused on their individual search intent.

## Business Restrictions
Bamboost does NOT currently provide or promote:
- Digital Marketing
- Performance Marketing
- Google Ads
- PPC
- Paid Advertising
- Google Ads Management

Do not target, promote, or add these as services.

## Dedicated Pages
Each page must have a clear independent purpose:
- SEO Company → broad commercial SEO intent
- Technical SEO → technical search infrastructure
- On-Page SEO → content/page-level optimization
- Off-Page SEO → authority/backlinks/off-site signals
- Local SEO → local visibility, Maps, Google Business Profile
- Ecommerce SEO → products, categories, ecommerce search visibility
- Hotel SEO → hospitality-specific SEO problems
- Education SEO → education-specific SEO problems
- Real Estate SEO → property/location-specific SEO problems
- Web Development → website development and technical implementation

Do not reuse the same generic introduction, benefits, process, or CTA wording across pages.

## Content Optimization Method
For every page:
1. Read the existing content completely.
2. Understand the search intent.
3. Identify the primary topic.
4. Identify duplicate content.
5. Identify generic agency language.
6. Identify unnecessary sections.
7. Remove weak/repetitive content.
8. Consolidate overlapping sections.
9. Rewrite around search intent.
10. Add only relevant semantic/entity concepts.
11. Improve content hierarchy.
12. Design the optimized content into useful sections.

Goal: higher information value, not higher word count.

## Design Quality
The result should feel premium, modern, professional, technical, trustworthy, clean, sophisticated, and conversion-focused.

Avoid:
- Excessive black space
- Huge empty sections
- Generic agency templates
- Too many cards
- Repetitive grids
- Excessive gradients
- Excessive glassmorphism
- Excessive animation
- Visual clutter
- Walls of text

Use whitespace intentionally.

## Technical Requirements
Use only:
- HTML
- CSS
- JavaScript
- Bootstrap 5.3+

Do not introduce React, Vue, Angular, Tailwind, or unnecessary frameworks.

Preserve existing functionality, assets, navigation, forms, JavaScript, and responsive behavior.

## Technical SEO

- Unique title
- Unique meta description
- One clear H1
- Logical H2/H3 structure
