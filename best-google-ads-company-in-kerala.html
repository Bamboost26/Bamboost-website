The Problem

Why Google Ads Spend Often Fails to Produce Leads

Google Ads is Google's pay-per-click advertising platform. Businesses bid to show ads when people search for specific keywords, and pay each time someone clicks. If you are new to the platform, our guide on how Google Ads works explains the auction in plain language.

When a campaign spends money but the phone stays quiet, the cause is rarely a single setting. It is usually a combination of the issues listed here, and most of them can be found in an account audit.

Spend Without Enough Enquiries

Clicks are being paid for, but few of them turn into calls, form submissions or sales.

Irrelevant Search Traffic

Broad keywords and missing negative keywords let ads appear for searches that will never become customers.

Weak Campaign Structure

Unrelated services grouped together make it hard to write relevant ads or control budget by priority.

Missing or Inaccurate Tracking

Without reliable conversion tracking, Google's bidding and your own decisions are based on guesswork.

Landing Pages That Don't Convert

Sending ad traffic to a slow or generic homepage loses visitors who were ready to enquire.

Ads and Pages Saying Different Things

When the ad promises one thing and the page talks about another, visitors leave and ad quality suffers.

Measuring Results

How We Measure Google Ads Performance

Conversion tracking records the actions that matter to your business, such as a form submission, phone call or purchase, and connects them to the ad and keyword that produced them. Without it, the metrics opposite cannot be trusted.

Results depend on your budget, the level of competition, the accuracy of tracking, the quality of your landing pages and factors such as pricing and how quickly enquiries are followed up. We do not promise a specific number of leads or a guaranteed return.

Qualified Leads and Sales
The enquiries, calls or orders that fit your target customer. This is the primary measure of success.
Conversion Rate
The percentage of ad clicks that become a lead or sale. It reflects both targeting and landing page quality.
Cost per Lead or Acquisition
Total ad spend divided by the number of conversions. It shows what each new enquiry or customer costs.
Click-Through Rate (CTR)
The percentage of people who click your ad after seeing it. It indicates how relevant the ad is to the search.
Average Cost per Click (CPC)
The average amount paid for each click, set by the ad auction, your bids and the quality of your ads.
Total Spend and ROAS
Total ad spend over the period and, where revenue is tracked reliably, return on ad spend: revenue divided by ad cost.
