What It Is

What Are Local SEO Services and Why Does Your Business Need Them?

Local SEO (Local Search Engine Optimization) is the process of optimizing your online presence so your business appears prominently when people nearby search for your products or services on Google.

Unlike traditional SEO that targets the entire web, local SEO services focus specifically on getting your business visible in Google Maps, the local 3-pack, and city-based searches right at the moment a nearby customer is ready to buy.

How Google Decides Who Shows Up Locally

1
Relevance - Does your business match what they searched?
2
Proximity - How close is your business to the searcher?
3
Prominence - How trusted and well-known is your business online?
