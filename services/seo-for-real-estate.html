Why Your Projects Aren't Getting Enquiries from Google

Buyers start their search on Google, but most builder websites never show up for the searches that matter. These are the gaps we find most often:

Portals rank above you.

Buyers find your project on a portal, listed right next to your competitors.

Project pages are too thin.

No K-RERA number, floor plans or possession date, so Google and buyers both move on.

No pages for the areas buyers search.

Buyers search by neighbourhood, like "villas in Chevayur". Without location pages, that traffic goes elsewhere.

Enquiries aren't tracked.

Without tracking calls, WhatsApp and forms, you can't see which project or page is working.

SEO for Real Estate vs Property Portals

Portals give quick reach. SEO builds a lead source you own. Most builders do best with both, but only one keeps working when the budget stops.

Property portals

  • Your lead is shared, often beside competing projects
  • You pay for listings, packages or leads
  • Visibility ends when the plan ends

Real estate SEO

  • Buyers contact you directly from your website
  • You invest in pages and rankings you keep
  • Rankings usually keep bringing visits after active work slows

Real Estate SEO: Frequently Asked Questions

Real estate SEO is the work of making a builder's, developer's or agent's website show up when buyers search Google, Google Maps and AI tools for property. It covers project pages, location pages, local SEO and technical fixes, so buyers find your listings directly instead of through a property portal.

Most real estate websites start to see ranking improvements in 2–3 months, and a steady flow of enquiries usually takes 4–6 months. The timeline depends on your site's current condition, how competitive your locations are and how many projects you sell. We show progress in a report every month, so you are never guessing.

SEO and portals do different jobs. MagicBricks and 99acres give fast visibility, but you pay for it, share buyers with competitors and lose it when you stop paying. SEO takes longer, but the enquiries come to your own website, the lead is yours alone and rankings usually keep bringing visits after active work slows. Many builders use both.
