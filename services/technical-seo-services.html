⚡ Problem 1

Slow Page Loading Speed

Google uses page speed as a direct ranking signal. Pages that take more than 3 seconds to load lose 53% of mobile visitors before they even see your content and rank significantly lower than faster competitors. Poor Google PageSpeed Insights scores directly suppress your search rankings.

Crawl Errors & Indexation Issues

Google Search Console crawl errors, incorrectly blocked pages, noindex tags applied to pages that should rank, and missing XML sitemaps all prevent Google from properly finding and indexing your content - meaning your pages literally cannot appear in search results.

Poor Mobile Performance

Google uses mobile-first indexing - meaning your mobile website's performance determines your rankings for all devices. Poor mobile usability, slow mobile load times, and unoptimized mobile layouts directly penalize your search visibility across every device type.

🔗 Problem 4

Broken Links & 404 Errors

Broken internal and external links waste Google's crawl budget, create dead-end navigation paths for users, and lose the link equity that should flow through your site. Every broken link is a small signal to Google that your website isn't properly maintained.

Failed Core Web Vitals

Google's Core Web Vitals (LCP, CLS, FID/INP) are direct ranking factors measuring real-world page experience. Websites that fail these benchmarks receive a measurable ranking penalty and miss out on the Page Experience ranking boost that passing sites receive.

📄 Problem 6

Poor Site Architecture

Websites with illogical URL structures, missing canonical tags, duplicate content from URL parameters, and inefficient internal linking waste crawl budget and dilute ranking signals - preventing your most important pages from accumulating the authority they need to rank.

Understanding Technical SEO

What Are Technical SEO Services and Why Do They Determine Your Rankings?

Technical SEO Explained Simply

Technical SEO focuses on the behind-the-scenes structure of your website that helps Google crawl, index, and understand your content effectively. It includes factors like page speed, mobile usability, site accessibility, and overall performance. Just like a physical shop needs a strong, functional building before showcasing products, your website needs a solid technical foundation to ensure both users and search engines can access and experience your content smoothly.

How Google Crawls and Indexes Websites

Google uses automated programs called crawlers (Googlebot) to discover, read, and evaluate web pages. These crawlers follow links between pages, read your content, and send information back to Google's index. Google Search Console provides data on how your site is being crawled, which pages are indexed, and what technical issues are preventing proper indexation. Our website optimization technical SEO process uses this data as the primary source of truth for identifying and fixing every issue affecting your search visibility.

"Technical SEO is not just about fixing errors - it's about building a website that Google can trust enough to rank prominently and users can rely on enough to convert."
🔍 What Google's Technical Evaluation Covers
SPEED Page load time, server response, and Core Web Vitals scores
CRAWL Robots.txt rules, XML sitemap accuracy, and crawl budget efficiency
INDEX Canonical tags, noindex settings, and duplicate content management
MOBILE Mobile optimization: mobile usability, responsive design, and mobile Core Web Vitals
SCHEMA Structured data validity and rich result eligibility
HTTPS Security certificate validity and mixed content errors

Core Web Vitals

Core Web Vitals - Google's Performance Ranking Signal That Most Websites Fail

Google's Core Web Vitals are a set of specific, measurable page experience metrics that Google uses as direct ranking signals. Unlike some ranking factors that are difficult to verify, Core Web Vitals are publicly measurable - you can check your scores right now in Google PageSpeed Insights or Google Search Console and websites that meet the "Good" threshold for all three metrics receive a meaningful ranking advantage over those that fail.

Most websites in Kerala fail at least one Core Web Vitals metric, and many fail all three. Our technical SEO optimization process targets all three benchmarks systematically, implementing the code-level changes needed to move your scores from Needs Improvement or Poor into the Good range - unlocking the Page Experience ranking benefit that passing sites receive.

LCP
Largest Contentful Paint

How fast your main content loads. Target: under 2.5 seconds. Affects your visual load perception rating.

Good: < 2.5s
CLS
Cumulative Layout Shift

How stable your page layout is as it loads. Prevents elements jumping around and disrupting users.

Good: < 0.1
INP
Interaction to Next Paint

How quickly your page responds to user interactions like clicks, taps, and key presses. Measures responsiveness.

Good: < 200ms
