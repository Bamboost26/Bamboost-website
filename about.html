Bamboost LLP is an SEO agency based in Kozhikode, Kerala, offering SEO, web development and Google Ads. We help businesses get found on Google, Google Maps and AI tools like ChatGPT and Google AI Overviews, and we report every month on the calls, WhatsApp messages and enquiries that come from it.

Why We Started Bamboost

Bamboost exists because too many businesses were paying agencies for activity, not results. Reports full of tasks and impressions, but no clear answer to the only question that matters: did more customers call?

So we built Bamboost around one principle: your growth is the measure. That means search traffic that turns into enquiries, and a website that does its job.

Why the name Bamboost?

Bamboo spends years building a root system you can't see before it grows fast above ground. That's how we approach SEO: we invest in the foundations most agencies skip, so growth is steady and built to last.
